Changes from this brief

  1. "Tailor" reads "Tailor CV": app language wants a verb and an object on every button, and it is still shorter than the detail page's "Tailor my CV".
  2. Tailor's ready label is "View CV", with the same FileText icon and no arrow: it opens a modal, not a new tab, so the up-right arrow would promise the wrong thing.
  3. The posting is a sample (Tidepool, Senior Full-stack Engineer): this canvas cannot read the live Job board. E5 uses a deliberately long title and company to test overflow. Swap in real postings at review.
  4. The score keeps its neutral outline badge and only grows: the figure is Geist Mono 20px semibold, the badge 36px tall. The ATS badge sits after it, one step smaller (28px), so the score stays the first thing read.

Proposals

No new token or component. The card actions reuse the shipped rainbow-ring class at Button size sm (32px), the same FileText and Mail icons stroked with the ring's gradient, Dialog, Textarea and the toaster.

The reorganization recommendation (E9)

E1 holds on desktop: the title keeps over 600px beside the score, ATS and Save, and the three footer actions fit on one line. Two things do read as crowded once drawn with real content, so I recommend a small restructure rather than a new card:

  • Split the footer by role. Tailor CV and Write cover letter sit together on the left; Apply stays alone on the right as the one primary action. In E1, three right-aligned buttons of near-equal weight compete with Apply.
  • Move pay into the footer, just left of Apply. Today pay sits on its own right-aligned line above the separator; folding it into the footer saves a row on every card, which pays for the new buttons.
  • On phones (E10), move score and ATS out of the header into a row under the company, so the title keeps its width. The footer wraps to two rows: the AI actions, then pay and Apply. Labels fit at 390, so no icon-only collapse is needed.

Open questions

  1. ATS wording (E2): "ATS 85%", "85% ATS" or "ATS match 85%". Recommend "ATS 85%": shortest, and the label before the figure mirrors "92 match" without copying it.
  2. Icons on the card buttons: recommend keeping FileText and Mail. The rainbow icon is what ties the card action to the detail page's, and it costs about 22px each, which fits.
  3. Explaining ATS: recommend a Tooltip on the badge, "6 of 7 posting skills in your Profile", since a bare percentage does not say what it counts.
  4. No skills on a posting: the spec hides the ATS badge. Recommend the score stays exactly where it is, so the header does not shift between cards.
