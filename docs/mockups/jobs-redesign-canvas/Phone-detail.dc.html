About the role

Tidepool builds scheduling and intake software used by more than 300 community clinics. We are a team of 22, fully remote, with engineers in Mexico, Colombia, Argentina and the Philippines.

You will own features end to end, from the database migration to the last pixel, and work with one designer and a product manager on a two-week cycle.

What you will do

  • Build patient and staff features in our Next.js app and the Node services behind it
  • Design PostgreSQL schemas and write the migrations that ship them
  • Review code and pair with two mid-level engineers
  • Take part in a light on-call rotation, about one week in eight

What we are looking for

  • 5 or more years building web products with React and TypeScript
  • Comfortable with Node.js, REST and GraphQL APIs
  • Experience with PostgreSQL in production
  • At least 4 hours of overlap with 9 AM to 5 PM Mexico City time

Nice to have

  • AWS (ECS, RDS, SQS)
  • Healthcare or HIPAA experience
